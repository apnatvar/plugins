# Privacy

PPDF processes PDF files on the user's device. The extension requests zero Chrome permissions, does not upload files, and has no server-side document processing. Compression and page operations run within the local extension using browser APIs and bundled libraries.

PPDF does not collect PDF contents for analytics or transmit them to Brownsmith Dynamics. Product and company links in the extension identify the publisher; they do not participate in document processing.

Encrypted PDFs are rejected. Large PDFs may use substantial local memory. Image-to-PDF conversion accepts JPG and PNG images up to 40 megapixels and 100 MB each. Page rewrites can affect PDF document-level features; forms, outlines, attachments, metadata, layers, and signatures may not be preserved reliably. Users should retain their source file when those features matter.
