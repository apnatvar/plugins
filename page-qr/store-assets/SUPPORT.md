# Page QR Support

**Publisher:** Brownsmith Dynamics  
**Email:** [contact@brownsmithdynamics.com](mailto:contact@brownsmithdynamics.com)

For help with Page QR, email the address above. Please include your Chrome version, operating system, and a description of what happened. Do not include a private page URL, account details, or a QR image unless you have checked that it contains nothing sensitive. Page QR codes preserve the complete URL, including query parameters and fragments.

Page QR reads the active HTTP or HTTPS tab's URL locally to create a QR code. It does not send the URL or image to Brownsmith Dynamics. If you choose to share the QR, your browser or operating system passes the image and full URL to the native share interface and the destination you select. Downloaded and copied PNGs can contain the complete URL.
